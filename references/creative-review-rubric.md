# Creative review rubric

Review each category from 1–5, then apply the weighting in SKILL.md.

- **Strategy/message:** Is the value clear? Is the idea rooted in a real consumer tension? Is the brand role clear?
- **Opening:** Does the first image create a reason to continue? Is the category context clear enough for the placement?
- **Story/emotion:** Is there an emotional progression? Are character actions motivated? Does the product matter to the story?
- **Product proof:** Are features clear? Is there visual evidence? Are claims and interactions accurate?
- **Visual craft:** Are lighting, palette, lens, texture, and composition coherent? Does the sequence feel like one film?
- **Sound/edit:** Does music support story and pace? Are SFX/foley credible? Do transitions and text help?
- **Brand/conversion:** Is the next action clear when needed? Is the brand memorable? Is the balance right for placement?

Always separate:
- **Must-fix:** factual errors, product distortion, unclear message, broken continuity, misleading claims, confusing edit, missing essential proof.
- **High-impact:** weak hook, slow pacing, insufficient product demonstration, poor CTA.
- **Optional polish:** minor grade, transition, grain, or sound refinements.
