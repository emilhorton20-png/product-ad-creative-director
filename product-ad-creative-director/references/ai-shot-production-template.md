## Product continuity checklist
- Silhouette and proportions unchanged.
- Correct component count and placement.
- Correct material, finish, color, and texture.
- Logo/text unchanged unless explicitly requested.
- Functional details remain plausible; no invented parts, seams, holes, controls, or accessories.
- Scale relative to hands/furniture/environment is credible.
- Contact shadows and reflections match the environment.

## Motion checklist
State what moves, what stays still, direction, speed, and duration. Keep camera and subject motion distinct. Avoid multiple complex actions in one shot. For image-to-video, preserve the starting composition unless reframing is intended.
